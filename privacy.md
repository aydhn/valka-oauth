# Privacy Policy - OYS Studio VALKA

Last updated: 8 November 2026

OYS Studio VALKA ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the VALKA YouTube channel on their own computer. It is used only to manage that one channel.

## What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these YouTube permissions:

- **youtube.upload** - to upload the channel's own videos as private videos.
- **youtube.force-ssl** - to set or move the scheduled publication time of the channel's own private videos.
- **youtube.readonly** - to check the channel's own videos and their status.
- **yt-analytics.readonly** - to read the channel's own view statistics.

## How the data is used

- The data is used only to upload, schedule and check videos on the owner's own channel.
- The app does not access any other channel, account or user.
- Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- No data is used to train AI or machine-learning models.

## Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never published. Video and channel data stay on that computer.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to the user support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
